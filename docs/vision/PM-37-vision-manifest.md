## Scope

The manifest documents the Tardis platform's living thesis, trust sequence, product language, and decision principles for Phase 2:

- **Thesis**: Metacognitive Business OS — agents that compose business verticals, not just chat
- **Trust sequence**: observability → explainability → controllability → autonomy
- **Product language**: compositions, projections, surfaces, blocks (vs. pages/portals)
- **Decision principles**: (a) explicit over implicit, (b) verifiable before autonomous, (c) compositional over monolithic
